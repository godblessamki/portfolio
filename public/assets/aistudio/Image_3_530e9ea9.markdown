## Brand & Style

This design system targets an uncompromising tier of creative technology, digital direction, and haute-couture interactive craft. Tailored for executive creative directors, elite studio founders, and visionary technologists, the aesthetic merges dark cinematic luxury with hyper-responsive kinetic feedback. The interface functions not merely as an archive of artifacts, but as an interactive gallery where every cursor vector and scroll friction metric communicates prestige and engineering authority.

Drawing from high-contrast editorial minimalism intertwined with kinetic dark-mode glassmorphism, the visual environment emphasizes atmospheric depth over decorative clutter. Surfaces read like layered obsidian crystal; illumination is earned entirely through interactive proximity, glowing accents, and delicate structural grid lines. The system evokes focused curiosity, awe, and effortless technical supremacy.

## Colors

The palette is engineered to construct deep spatial hierarchy through light-absorbent backdrops contrasted with laser-precise optical illumination:

- **Surface Base (`#090A0F`)**: Deepest void black; absorbs ambient layout space and establishes infinite depth.
- **Surface Elevation (`#0E1017`)**: Dense slate obsidian; used for contextual card backings and secondary panels.
- **Surface High (`#161926`)**: Translucent structural fill for interactive targets and active modal layers.
- **Primary Kinetic Accent (`#8A5CFF`)**: Iridescent violet used for primary kinetic energy states, high-priority interactive cues, and dominant cursor blooms.
- **Secondary Accent (`#4D38F5`)**: Electric indigo; functions as an anchoring counter-weight in gradients, subtle border illumination, and structural framing.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#4EE0FF`)**: Icy cyan; strictly applied to pinpoint data indicators, micro-metrics, precision crosshairs, and live status signals.
- **Text & Foregrounds**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) for razor-sharp display titles, Ice Mist (`#A8B0CD`) for secondary editorial passages, and Muted Starlight (`#58607A`) for metadata, technical stamps, and tertiary footnotes.

## Typography

The typographic tension relies on three distinct voices working in counterpoint:

1. **Syne (Display & Headlines)**: A sculptural, high-contrast sans-serif that infuses an avant-garde runway feel. Wide curves and razor terminals demand attention across project titles and oversized billboard anchors.
2. **Geist (Body & Narrative)**: A surgically disciplined, neutral sans-serif designed for pristine legibility at micro and standard sizes against deep dark-mode backgrounds.
3. **Space Mono (Metadata, Indicators & Badges)**: Precise, technical, and computational. Used for year stamps, client codes, coordinates, and role definitions to preserve the feeling of a digital director’s operating terminal.

Always balance expansive display typography with generous breathing room. For all typography roles labeled under `label-*`, ensure text transforms to uppercase to maintain structural consistency.

## Layout & Spacing

The interface deploys a flexible 12-column responsive grid underpinned by an 8pt modular rhythm:

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns with `2.5rem` gutters and `4rem` outer boundary margins. Hero treatments and showcase carousels can break margin barriers using viewport bleed anchors (`100vw`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` screen margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` screen margins. Multi-column editorial compositions collapse gracefully to linear, single-column stacks.

Structural grid lines are faintly demarcated across desktop viewports using 1px stroke lines tinted with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`, illuminated to `rgba(138, 92, 255, 0.15)` dynamically on cursor trajectory proximity. Generous vertical intervals (`space-2xl`, `space-3xl`) isolate major case study movements to enforce dramatic pacing.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through luminous volumetric layering and optical refraction rather than legacy opacity drops:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base)**: Matte `#090A0F` background layered with an ultra-fine 1.5% SVG film noise to prevent color banding across deep gradients.
- **Level 1 (Structural Cards & Modules)**: Background tinted at `#0E1017` with a hair-thin perimeter boundary of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`. No heavy dropshadows; depth relies on tonal separation.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Nav & Overlays)**: Glassmorphism via `backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(160%)`, coupled with a translucent fill of `rgba(14, 16, 23, 0.65)` and an ambient, low-spread glow: `0 16px 40px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0 0 20px -2px rgba(138, 92, 255, 0.15)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal & Media Lightbox Focus)**: High-altitude z-index with a dual backdrop: an obscuring deep-space scrim (`rgba(9, 10, 15, 0.88)` with `blur(32px)`) topped with a luminous rim light (`1px solid rgba(78, 224, 255, 0.3)`).
- **Proximity Bloom**: Interactive components produce dynamic radial gradients (`rgba(138, 92, 255, 0.12)`) calculated via pointer tracking, creating a living halo beneath the user's cursor.

## Shapes

The design system embraces high-roundedness (Level 3: Pill-Shaped). This curvature directly contrasts with the crisp, architectural grid lines and monolithic typography:

- **Floating Navigation, Tags & Interactive CTAs**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to establish a distinct tactile presence that separates toolbars from media canvases.
- **Showcase Cards & Media Viewports**: `rounded-2xl` (1.5rem / 24px) on desktop, tapering to `rounded-xl` (1rem / 16px) on mobile viewports to conserve screen real estate.
- **Micro-Indicators & Tech Badges**: Subtle rounded capsules (0.5rem / 8px) balancing the monospaced typographic density with smooth contours.

## Components

### Floating Navigation Pill
- Centered dock pinned `2rem` above the bottom or top viewport edge.
- Glassmorphic body: pill-radius, `rgba(14, 16, 23, 0.7)` background, `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, `blur(20px)`.
- Nav links styled in `Space Mono` (`label-md`). Hover triggers a luminous kinetic pill highlight beneath the text using an ease-out spring physics curve.

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary CTA**: Pill shape, rich dark interior with a continuous animated gradient border (iridescent violet to electric indigo). Text rendered in crisp white `Geist` medium with dynamic forward arrow micro-icon.
- **Secondary Action**: Borderless glass capsule (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`) with hover illumination expanding a subtle cyan tint.
- **Tactile State**: `transform: scale(0.97)` on active click with an instantaneous 100ms rebound.

### Case Study Cards
- Obsidian glass container with 16:9 or 4:5 cinematic media masking.
- Default state: Media muted at 80% opacity with hairline border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.
- Cursor Hover State: Cursor changes to an oversized magnetic magnetic glass disc reading "EXPLORE". Media scales smoothly to 103% over 600ms, border highlights to `rgba(138, 92, 255, 0.4)`, and card produces a subtle outward indigo shadow bloom.

### Metadata Chips & Status Badges
- Pill-shaped miniature containers with `Space Mono` typography (`label-sm`).
- Features a pulsing 6px kinetic LED dot on active statuses: `box-shadow: 0 0 8px #4EE0FF`.

### Lists & Archive Index
- Minimal horizontal dividers (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`).
- Hovering an archive item triggers a floating visual thumbnail preview that tracks the pointer along the item row, crossfading project metadata in real time.

### Input Fields & Inquiry Terminal
- Minimalist underline or deep dark capsule (`#0E1017`).
- Active focus smoothly ignites a perimeter glow in iridescent violet (`#8A5CFF`), shifting placeholder typography from `Muted Starlight` to `Ice Mist`.
